After Your Treatment

Sculptra Aftercare & Recovery

A calm, complete guide to caring for the treated area — including the important massage routine — and how your natural collagen result unfolds over the months ahead.

Sculptra works by stimulating your own collagen. A little swelling straight after is from the fluid it's mixed with and settles within days — your real result develops gradually over 6–12 weeks and builds across your course of sessions.

Every person heals differently. The first days are not your final result — trust the process, do your massage, and never hesitate to reach out to us.

The most important step — your massage ("Rule of 5s")

Gently massaging the treated area helps distribute the product evenly and reduces the risk of lumps and nodules. The simple way to remember it:

Massage the area 5 times a day,
for about 5 minutes each time,
for 5 days after treatment.

How to massage

  • Use clean hands on clean skin
  • Gentle, firm circular pressure across the whole treated area
  • Spread it evenly — don't focus on one spot
  • Clean moisturiser can help hands glide

Please note

  • Your practitioner's exact instructions take precedence over the general "5s" rule
  • The buttock / body protocol may differ — follow what you were told for your area
  • Don't massage so hard that it hurts or bruises

If you're ever unsure how or when to massage, message us — we'd always rather you asked.

What is completely normal?

In the first few days it is completely normal to experience:

Normal & expected

  • Swelling, bruising & redness
  • Tenderness & mild aching
  • Small firm areas or lumps (often settle with massage)
  • The area looking fuller at first, then settling
  • A feeling that "nothing much happened" after week 1 — this is expected, as the result is gradual
Don't judge your result early. Any early plumpness is just fluid and settles. Your collagen builds gradually over 6–12 weeks and across your sessions.

The first 24–72 hours

Please do

  • Do your massage ("rule of 5s" or as instructed)
  • Apply a clean cool compress for swelling (10 mins at a time, not ice directly on skin)
  • Stay well hydrated
  • Keep the area clean

Please avoid

  • Heat — sauna, steam room, hot yoga, very hot baths/showers
  • Strenuous exercise for 24–72 hours
  • Makeup on the area for the first 24 hours (facial treatment)
  • Alcohol for 24–48 hours

Hygiene

Keeping the area clean

  • Always wash your hands before touching or massaging the area
  • Keep the skin clean; a gentle cleanser is fine after the first 24 hours
  • Avoid swimming pools, hot tubs and saunas for the first few days (infection risk)
  • For buttock/body treatment, keep clothing clean and loose over the area

Face vs body — a few differences

Face

  • Sleep on your back where you can; avoid pressing the treated area into the pillow for a few nights
  • Skip makeup on the area for the first 24 hours
  • Daily SPF supports your skin and result

Body — especially buttocks

  • Avoid prolonged sitting or direct pressure on the buttocks where you can for the first 24–72 hours (stand, walk, or lie on your front / side)
  • Avoid tight jeans, shapewear and restrictive underwear early on
  • Wear loose, comfortable clothing
  • Follow the specific massage instructions given for your area

Managing tenderness

You may

  • Take Paracetamol if you need pain relief
  • Rest the area
  • Use a clean cool compress for swelling

Avoid (unless prescribed)

  • Ibuprofen
  • Aspirin
  • Other anti-inflammatories (NSAIDs)

Mild inflammation is part of the collagen-building process, so we suggest paracetamol rather than anti-inflammatories, unless your prescriber has advised otherwise.

Heat, exercise & activity

Activity guide

  • First 24–72 hours: avoid strenuous exercise, gym, running & heavy lifting
  • Heat: avoid sauna, steam, hot yoga and very hot baths for the first few days
  • Gentle walking is encouraged and helps swelling settle
  • Return to your normal routine gradually after 2–3 days, as comfortable

Your review & next session

Sculptra is a course, so your next session is usually around 4–6 weeks later. We'll review how your collagen is responding, compare photographs, and plan the next step. Please keep your appointments so your result builds evenly.

  • Persistent or increasing lumpiness or unevenness
  • Swelling or redness that isn't settling after several days
  • An area that stays tender or firmer than expected
  • Any symptom that's worrying you

Seek urgent help — signs of a serious problem

Please seek urgent medical care (call 999 or attend A&E) and contact us if you experience:
  • Signs of vascular occlusion (very rare): severe or increasing pain out of proportion, skin turning white (blanching), or grey/blue/purple/mottled, skin becoming cold or blistering
  • Signs of infection: spreading redness or heat, pus or discharge, a lump that becomes red, hot & painful, fever or feeling unwell
  • A painful, red or rapidly worsening nodule
  • Any difficulty breathing, facial swelling or feeling seriously unwell (possible allergic reaction) — call 999 immediately
If it is severe or you feel very unwell, call 999 / attend A&E first, then let us know. For anything else that concerns you, call 07787 708200.

A final reassurance

Most side effects after Sculptra are temporary and part of normal healing. The vast majority of clients heal without complication and gradually enjoy firmer, fuller, more natural-looking results over the months following treatment.
