What to expect — gradual result

Sculptra is a collagen-stimulating treatment, not an instant filler. It works by prompting your body to build its own new collagen over time.

Please understand before you proceed

  • Any plumpness in the first days is from fluid and settles — it is not your result.
  • Your result develops gradually over 6–12 weeks and builds across sessions.
  • Sculptra is a course — typically 2–4 sessions, 4–6 weeks apart (body/buttock usually needs more).
  • Results vary between individuals and are not guaranteed.
  • Results are long-lasting (commonly up to ~2 years) but not permanent; top-ups maintain them.

Risks & complications

No injectable treatment is entirely risk-free. Sculptra is well established and generally very safe, but the following risks may occur, and there may be unforeseen risks not listed.

Common / temporary

  • Swelling, bruising, redness
  • Tenderness & mild pain
  • Small early lumps or bumps
  • Itching at injection sites
  • Temporary firmness in the area

Less common / serious

  • Granuloma (a persistent inflammatory lump)
  • Visible or palpable lumpiness / unevenness
  • Infection or abscess
  • Vascular occlusion (filler blocking a blood vessel) — rarer with PLLA than HA, but possible; can cause tissue damage
  • Nerve irritation or altered sensation
  • Asymmetry or under-/over-correction needing further treatment
  • Allergic / hypersensitivity reaction (rare)

Delayed-onset nodules & papules — the key Sculptra risk

The most important Sculptra-specific risk is delayed-onset nodules or papules — small firm lumps that can appear weeks or months after treatment. Most are minor and treatable when reported early. The risk is reduced by correct technique, correct dilution, and by you following the massage instructions (see below).

Your massage commitment (rule of 5s)

Massaging the treated area after Sculptra helps spread the product evenly and reduces the risk of lumps and nodules. The general rule is the "rule of 5s":

The rule of 5s

  • Massage 5 times a day
  • For about 5 minutes each time
  • For 5 days after treatment
  • Your practitioner's exact instructions take precedence, and the buttock/body protocol may differ

Vascular occlusion & emergencies

A vascular occlusion — filler blocking or compressing a blood vessel — is rare with Sculptra but is a medical emergency. Possible signs include:

  • Severe or increasing pain out of proportion to what's expected
  • White skin (blanching)
  • Grey, blue, purple or mottled discolouration
  • Skin becoming cold, or blistering
  • Signs of infection: spreading redness/heat, pus, fever, feeling unwell
  • Any breathing difficulty, facial swelling or feeling very unwell (seek emergency help immediately)

I understand that if any of these occur I must contact Layali Clinic immediately (07787 708200) or, if severe, call 999 / attend A&E.
